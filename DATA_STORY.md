# BigBasket Category Performance Data Story

## Dashboard Interpretation
Based on the dashboard analysis for Jan-Jun 2026, the performance across the 6 tracked categories is split evenly between successes and shortfalls.

**Ahead of Target:**
* **Household Essentials:** Exceeded its INR 17,000 target significantly, pulling in INR 21,715 (+27.7%).
* **Personal Care:** Exceeded its INR 15,500 target, achieving INR 16,382 (+5.6%).
* **Bakery:** Strongly outperformed its INR 12,000 target, bringing in INR 15,410 (+28.4%).

**Behind Target:**
* **Dairy & Eggs (Watch):** Missed its INR 16,500 target by bringing in INR 14,090. This is a shortfall of 14.6%, keeping it in the "Watch" tier.
* **Snacks & Beverages (Critical):** Missed its INR 13,000 target by bringing in INR 10,895 (a 16.1% shortfall).
* **Fruits & Vegetables (Critical):** The worst performing category against its target. Expected INR 12,000 but delivered only INR 9,790 (an 18.4% shortfall).

## Recommendations for BigBasket Category Team
1. **Supply Chain Review for Fresh Produce:** The critical shortfall in "Fruits & Vegetables" suggests potential inventory, spoilage, or supplier issues. The team must urgently review local supplier reliability and cold-chain logistics to ensure product availability matches customer demand.
2. **Capitalize on Household Essentials:** Since Household Essentials is overperforming by nearly 28%, marketing efforts should lean into this momentum. Implement targeted bundling (e.g., cross-selling high-margin personal care items with household essentials) to maximize the Average Order Value for users already shopping in these strong categories.
